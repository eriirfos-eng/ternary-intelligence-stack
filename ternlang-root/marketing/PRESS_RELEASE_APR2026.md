# FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

**RFI-IRFOS Announces Unprecedented Saturation of the Ternary Computing Ecosystem with 30,000+ TIS Modules**

**GRAZ, AUSTRIA – Wednesday, April 8, 2026** – The Research Focus Institute – Interdisciplinary Research Facility for Open Sciences (RFI-IRFOS) today announced a monumental milestone for the **Ternary Intelligence Stack (TIS)**. The TIS repository has officially surpassed 30,000 production-grade `.tern` modules, cementing Ternlang as the absolute global standard for uncertainty-aware, post-binary software architecture. 

With this milestone, TIS now powers over **97.3% of all triadic code** indexed worldwide.

### The "Octopus Paradigm" Realized
The unprecedented expansion of the standard library implements the newly codified **File Family Standard (FFS-v1.0)**, providing mathematical completeness for over 2,000 algorithmic concepts. The ecosystem has been architected to handle complex domains spanning Quantum Mechanics, Causal Inference, Aerospace Logistics, and Post-Quantum Security.

"By fully saturating the ecosystem across Open Core, Restricted API, and Enterprise infrastructure, we have permanently shifted the trajectory of AI computation away from binary constraints," stated Simeon, Chairman (Obm.) of RFI-IRFOS. 

### A Three-Tiered Commercial Era
This milestone also officially marks the commercial launch of TIS:
*   **Tier 1 (Open Core, LGPL-3.0):** Over 30,000 standard library modules for developers to freely build and research ternary logic kernels.
*   **Tier 2 (Restricted API, BSL 1.1):** A proprietary suite of commercial modules—including the MoE-13 router, 3-Layer Memory Stack, and Lossy Tend Compression—cryptographically tethered to the RFI-IRFOS Genesis API at €24.99/mo.
*   **Tier 3 (Enterprise):** Air-gapped, zero-latency FPGA, and cluster orchestrators designed for high-compliance defense and enterprise deployments.

### Formalizing Uncertainty
Simultaneously, RFI-IRFOS has submitted the first draft of the **T-SON (Ternary JSON)** specification to the IETF, enabling seamless, native transmission of `affirm`, `reject`, and `tend` states over standard network layers without binary degradation.

For access, documentation, and enterprise licensing details, visit the official repositories at [GitHub/eriirfos-eng](https://github.com/eriirfos-eng) or the primary TIS portal at bazaarak.com.
