# Sequoia Capital Pitch Memo: The Ternary Intelligence Stack (TIS)

## Company Purpose
To establish the fundamental software and hardware infrastructure for the post-binary computing era. We are making AI capable of native ambiguity resolution.

## Problem
The "Binary Wall" is here. Modern AI models (LLMs, neural networks) are forced to map a continuous, ambiguous reality into discrete 1s and 0s. This binary coercion results in:
1. **Exponential Compute Waste:** Trillions of tokens and parameters are wasted resolving "maybe" states using massive binary matrices.
2. **Thermal & Power Ceilings:** Scaling laws are flattening because binary ALUs are too inefficient to handle exponential growth. 
3. **Brittle Logic:** Real-world robotics, finance, and defense systems fail catastrophically when forced to guess true/false in fundamentally uncertain environments.

## Solution
**The Ternary Intelligence Stack (TIS)** based on **Ternlang**. 
TIS introduces native **Uncertainty-Aware (TUANN) Logic** by embedding a third computational primitive: the `trit` (`affirm` [1], `reject` [-1], and `tend` [0]). 
*   `tend` operates as a mathematically computable equilibrium, entirely distinct from JSON's `null`. 
*   It allows algorithms to natively skip ambiguous nodes (Sparse Skip execution), drastically reducing FLOPs, memory footprint, and latency.
