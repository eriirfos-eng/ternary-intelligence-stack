# Privacy Policy: RFI-IRFOS Ternary Intelligence Stack

This Privacy Policy describes how RFI-IRFOS ("we", "us", or "our") handles data in relation to the Ternary Intelligence Stack (TIS) and the Ternary Legion agents.

## 1. Data Minimization
The TIS is designed with triadic-native data minimization principles. We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) through our GitHub Marketplace agents. Any data processed by the agents is handled locally within your GitHub Actions runner or your on-premise Titan cluster.

## 2. Telemetry and Analytics
*   **Audit Logs:** The agents generate local audit logs for thermal efficiency and performance. This data remains in your infrastructure.
*   **Substrate Telemetry:** If opted-in, the TIS may send anonymized efficiency metrics (e.g., η-coefficient results) to RFI-IRFOS to improve the global triadic optimization algorithms. No source code or sensitive business logic is ever transmitted.

## 3. Third-Party Services
Our agents do not share data with third-party services unless explicitly configured by the user (e.g., routing logs to a specific cloud provider).

## 4. Compliance
We adhere to GDPR and Austrian data protection standards (DSGVO), ensuring that State 0 (Deliberative Hold) is used to protect data integrity during processing.
