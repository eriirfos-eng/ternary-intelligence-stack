# Support: RFI-IRFOS Ternary Intelligence Stack

RFI-IRFOS is committed to providing expert support for all "Ternary Legion" agents and the full Ternary Intelligence Stack (TIS) ecosystem.

## 1. Support Channels
*   **Email Support:** For all technical inquiries, please contact **enterprise@rfi-irfos.org**. We prioritize requests from Tier-2 and Tier-3 subscribers.
*   **GitHub Issues:** For bug reports and feature requests related to our open-core components (Tier-1), please use the [official GitHub Issues tracker](https://github.com/eriirfos-eng/ternary-intelligence-stack/issues).

*   **Tier-1 (Free):** Best effort. Our community-driven support team monitors issues during business hours (CET).
*   **Tier-2 (€24.99/mo):** 24-hour response time for critical substrate-level integration issues.
*   **Tier-3 (Titan):** 1-hour response time with a dedicated technical oversight team and monthly efficiency audits.
