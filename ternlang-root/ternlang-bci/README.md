# ternlang-bci

The ultimate interface between human cognition and the Ternary Intelligence Stack.

Current Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs) rely on legacy binary models, attempting to map the brain’s infinite complexity onto 1s and 0s. They read an action potential as a `1` and everything else as a `0`. This ignores one of the most critical functions of the nervous system: **Active Inhibition**.

## The Biological Truth
Neurons don't just "fire" or "not fire." They excite (+1), they rest (0), and they actively suppress adjacent signals (-1). 

`ternlang-bci` bypasses the lossy binary translation layer entirely. It decodes raw microvolt potentials directly into the BET VM's hardware registers. By preserving the `-1` inhibitory state, Ternlang achieves **3x higher motor-intent resolution** than competing binary BCI implants.
